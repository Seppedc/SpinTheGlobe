# Spin The Globe — Economy & Skill Tree

The numbers live in `Data/Balance/GameBalance.asset` and `Data/SkillTree/SkillTree.asset`. The asset is generated by `DefaultSkillTreeFactory`. Pacing was checked with `Tools/economy_sim.py`; run it again after changing prices.

## Income sources
| Source | Formula | Typical share |
|---|---|---|
| Globe turn | €2.4 × income scale per revolution, paid in 12 small ticks (every 30°) | early game, lots of small payouts |
| Pin pass | €3 × location × pin payout × income scale | **~65%** |
| Case approved | max(€40 × case# × all income, **12 s of recent income**) × case reward | ~30% |
| Lootbox money | 30–80% refund, or a 2–4× jackpot (25%) | random |
| Paper ball in the bin | €25 × income scale | small |
| Statue smash | **gold** 10 × statue# × statue gold | gold |
| Lootbox gold | 2–8 gold | gold |
| ★ Press Coverage | 2% chance per approved case (up to 10% with Front Page News) to drop 5 × (1 + case#/10) gold | gold |

- *Income scale* = Globe earnings × All income.
- *Location*: sea ×1, land ×2, near a city up to ×6.
- *Recent income* is a 30-second moving average of what the globe and pins earn. This keeps cases worth doing all game without letting them explode, which a fixed ×1.6 per case did.
- **Cases:** need 3 + 2 × case# evidence, up to 40. The required pin durability goes up by 1 every 4 cases, up to 5. Cooldown 30 s.
- **Lootbox price:** €150 × 1.12^opened × lootbox price stat.

## Stat formula
final = (base + Σ add) × max(0.05, 1 + Σ percent) × Π (multiply^rank) × temporary buffs (whiskey)

## Skill tree (65 nodes, money only; the ★ items below now live in the Gold shop)
Costs are written as *base × growth per rank*. ★ nodes cost gold.

**Globe**
- Clockwork Motor (first node after the root): auto-rotate at +20% of max speed per rank (100% at rank 5). 5 ranks, €8 ×2.2.
- Greased Bearings (after Clockwork Motor): ×1.2 max speed per rank, compounding: 15 RPM base → ~575 RPM at rank 20 (about 10× the old 50 RPM top). 20 ranks, €10 ×2.3. Drag and click impulses scale with max speed (÷ 90°/s), so hand spinning stays useful.
- Firm Grip: +30% drag. 5 ranks, €20 ×1.8.
- Flick of the Wrist: +20% click. 5 ranks, €20 ×1.8.
- **Rule:** dragging always beats clicking. At the same upgrade level drag power ≥ click power, and every drag gives at least 1.5 clicks' worth of speed (checked by a unit test).
- Cartographer's Cut: +25% globe earnings. 25 ranks, €150 ×1.42.
- Gilded Axis: ×2 globe earnings. 1 rank, €5M.
- ★ Golden Meridian: ×1.5 all income. 150 gold.
- Globe Mastery: ×1.05 globe earnings per rank, endless. €2M ×1.25.

**Pins**
- Pin n: €20 × 3.6^(n−1), except Pin 1 at €10 (fast first shot). Pin 3 €259, Pin 6 €12K, Pin 9 €560K, Pin 12 €26M.
- Pin n · Durability: +1 pass. 5 ranks, 1.5 × the pin's price, ×1.6 per rank.
- Pin n · Payout: +50% for that pin. 10 ranks, 1.25 × the pin's price, ×1.45 per rank.
- Oiled Spring: −6% reload. 15 ranks (up to −90%), €25 ×1.45.
- Steady Aim: +8% land chance. 5 ranks, €80 ×1.9.
- Big City Lights: +5% city chance. 5 ranks, €200 ×2.
- Auto-loader: fires pins automatically. €50K.
- ★ Steel Tips: +1 durability for all pins. 60 gold.
- ★ Twin Barrel: 20% chance to fire 2 pins. 120 gold.
- Pin Mastery: ×1.05 all pin payouts per rank, endless. €5M ×1.25.

**Case file**
- Bigger Retainers: +25% reward. 10 ranks, €80 ×1.7.
- Efficient Paperwork: −7% cooldown. 10 ranks, €100 ×1.7.
- Rubber-Stamp Clerk: +5% auto-stamp. 10 ranks, €250 ×1.7.
- Cold Cases: −1 evidence needed. 3 ranks, €1K ×3.
- ★ Press Coverage: 2% chance an approved case drops gold. 40 gold.
- Front Page News: +1% gold-drop chance, 8 ranks (max 10% total, hard cap). €5K ×1.9.
- Case Mastery: ×1.05 case reward per rank, endless. €1M ×1.25.

**Statues**
- Molten Pour: +20% gold fill. 10 ranks, €150 ×1.6.
- Heavier Gold: +25% statue gold. 10 ranks, €250 ×1.6.
- ★ Fast Casting: −2 pieces per statue. 250 gold.
- Statue Mastery: ×1.05 statue gold per rank, endless. €1M ×1.25.

**Lootbox**
- Bulk Order: −6% price. 5 ranks, €300 ×2.
- Lucky Charm: +20% odds for statue pieces and gold. 5 ranks, €500 ×2.
- ★ Double Box: 15% chance of two rewards. 100 gold.
- Lootbox Mastery: +5% luck per rank, endless. €1M ×1.25.

**Whiskey**
- Slow Sipper: +2 s boost. 10 ranks, €100 ×1.55.
- Quick Refill: −6% cooldown. 10 ranks, €120 ×1.55.
- Top Shelf: +0.25× strength. 5 ranks, €400 ×2.2.
- ★ Bottomless Glass: refills itself. 200 gold.
- Whiskey Mastery: +1 s boost per rank, endless. €1M ×1.25.

★ nodes add up to about 920 gold. Gold sinks give the loop *money → lootbox → statue pieces → smash → gold → golden nodes* its purpose.

## Prestige and Gold shop
- **Retire the agency** (Gold shop, top card): possible once €10M is earned in the current run. Gold = floor(25 × √(run earnings ÷ €10M)): €10M → 25, €40M → 50, €100M → 79. Simulated first retirement: ~1 h.
- Resets money, the skill tree, pins, cases, whiskey, lootbox count and items. Keeps gold, the Gold shop, statues, settings and the lamp.
- **Skill tree is money only (65 nodes).** Statue nodes stay hidden until First Pedestal is bought; Front Page News until Press Coverage is bought.
- **Gold shop** (G, or the GOLD SHOP button):
  - Unlocks: First Pedestal 25 · Second Pedestal 75 · Third Pedestal 150 · Press Coverage 40.
  - Permanent: Agency Reputation +10% all income, 50 ranks, 20 ×1.35 · Golden Meridian ×1.5, 150 · Steel Tips 60 · Twin Barrel 120 · Double Box 100 · Bottomless Glass 200 · Fast Casting 250.
  - Fresh start (applied after retiring): Kept Ledger 10 (root unlocked) · Wound Clockwork 20 (Clockwork Motor rank 3) · Spare Pin 30 (Pin 1) · Seed Money 15 ×2.6, 5 ranks (€100 → €1M start).
- **Statue tiers** (Gold shop, per statue, after its pedestal): bronze → silver → gold. Each tier: +1 piece needed, gold ×2.5 (silver) / ×6 (gold). Costs: statue 1 60 → 198, statue 2 150 → 495, statue 3 300 → 990. Names, multipliers and extra pieces live in GameBalance.
- **Detective ranks** (GameBalance.ranks, add more in the inspector): Rookie 0 retirements · Detective 1 (+10% all income) · Senior Detective 3 (+25%) · Inspector 5 (+50%) · Chief Inspector 8 (+100%) · Commissioner 12 (+200%) · Legend of the Bureau 20 (+400%). The bonus is the rank's own value, not cumulative. Shown under the GOLD SHOP button and in the shop header.
- Lootboxes drop no statue pieces until a statue is unlocked (they pay money instead).
- Old saves: owned ★ nodes become their Gold shop items; statues that already had pieces get their pedestal unlock.
